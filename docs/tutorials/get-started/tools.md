# Tools

Essential tools for building mecha (or really any) EVA foam cosplay, listed roughly in order of importance.

## Essential Tools

### Exacto Knife
A stapled in your arsenal, and your primary tool for cutting out foam. I personally buy a pack of a hundred blades and swap out my blade often- others cosplayers use sharpeners for a similar effect. 

### Dremel/Rotary Tool
Once you have this, every problem looks like a dremel-shaped nail. The ability to swap out attachments means it can do anything from grinding down foam bevels to cutting metal stock. My #1 use of this tool is for cutting in EVA foam bevels with a sanding wheel and cleaning up seams/fuzzies with a grinding stone.

### Heat Gun
Nessecary to heat seal foam- also helps with shaping and bending foam and reheating pizza. A hairdryer will NOT work- they don't get hot enough to seal the foam.
- **Recommendation:** Whatever cheap heatgun you can find from Amazopn or Home Depot. 

### Ruler
For marking and general measuring, I recommend having a ruler with a transparent grid, to make squaring up perpendicular lines a breeze. It also helps when you need to offset a line by a specific amount. Having a long metal ruler to help guide your blade on long straight cuts can help as well.
-  **Recommendation:** A cheap, [long metal ruler](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Empire-36-in-Aluminum-Straight-Edge-Ruler-403/100185157) from the hardware store and a [graph ruler with a grid](https://www.amazon.com/Beveled-Transparent-Plastic-Measuring-Clothes/dp/B07RXX4DGV) on it will be almost all you need- maybe a sqaure ruler if you're feeling particularly fancy.

### White Gel Pens
The best way to write on black EVA foam.

- **Recommendation:** Sakura Gelly Roll White (Whatever cheap white gel pen will do the trick)

### Blue Ball Point
The cheap way to write on black EVA foam - harder to see, but is almost free. It also doesn't bleed through paint layers unlike some other ball point pen colors.

- **Recommendation:** The cheapest big box of blue Bics you can find.

### PVC Pipe Cutter
Makes cutting pipes quick and easy. Not "mandatory" - you could get away with using a cutoff wheel for a dremel. However, if you can swing it, it definitely makes life easier.

- **Recommendation:** Whatever you can get cheaply at home depot - these all seem to work the same.
