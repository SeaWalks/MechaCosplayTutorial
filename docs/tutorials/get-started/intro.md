## Before You Start

Building a Gundam/Mecha cosplay aint easy. Here are a couple of things you must think about before diving in. 

### Time

- Everyone works at their own pace with different amounts of free time each week to dedicate to cosplay. I would allocate **6 months to a year** to complete a build. 
- My builds take **many hundreds of hours**.  
- It is almost always better to delay a build than rush it and be unhappy with the result.

### Cost

- I would budget roughly **$400–$500 in materials**, plus ~$100 in tools if starting from scratch. Thankfully, you definitely won't need it all up front. While the total amount can vary wildly, thats the rough ballpark for a standard sized gundam build. 
- Costs are often backloaded into **painting and finishing**; Plastidip is an  `1 absolute killer.

### Handlers

You will need a “handler” — a friend (or two, or three) to help you survive the convention. This is a huge ask- these people will be chained to you, and you will suffer and die without them. 
They can help with:  

- Suiting up  
- Drinking water  
- Navigating the convention safely  
- Checking for damage / doing spot repairs  
- Manging your con badge
- Holding your stuff
- And a million other things that I take for granted

>**Tip:** If you can find a handler that is a cosplayer/cosplay adjacent, that would help a ton as they would be better equipped to do things like spot repairs and check for damage.

### Physical Demands

- Wearing a Gundam cosplay can be tiring, uncomfortable, or honestly straight up painful. It can be hot, heavy, hard to see in and annoying to move in. Make sure you are ready to face these challenges. 
>This varies wildly on the suit design; Thunderbolt Gundam nearly broke me, while Build Burning was a stroll through a rose garden.  
- This tutorial emphasizes wearability whenever possible, but big armor will always have limits. 

### Mental Demands

- Building a Gundam cosplay ~~can be~~ is mentally taxing. You will labor for hundreds of hours. A good portion of those probably won't  be fun. You will need to be highly motivated and be willing to trudge through the greatest of slogs. If you feel yourself burning out, maybe chose a smaller project or give yourself  time to take a break and fall back in love with the idea of building something awesome. 
> Don't forget- we're all in this together. Misery loves company, after all. 

### Transportation

- You need to **safely transport your suit**.  
- Most sedans can fit an average Gundam cosplay- crazier designs (big guns, big backpacks) might have to be transported in larger vehicles.
- Size varies by design, so always check beforehand.
